The doctoral programme at Hiroshima University runs from September 2026 to September 2029, under
Prof. Makoto Chikaraishi. The research is about **constraint heterogeneity in dynamic choice
models**: what it costs to let the constraints a person faces differ from person to person, and
which of those differences genuinely need their own computation. The full framing is on
[the research page](/).

> [!note] Status, 13 October 2026
> The direction is being taken to a supervisor session, not settled by it. Pages here are added
> only when there is something real to put on them, and each carries its date. Newest first.
