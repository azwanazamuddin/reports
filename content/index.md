Technical reports and research notes from my PhD work at
[Hiroshima University](https://www.hiroshima-u.ac.jp/en), in the
[Urban and Transportation Planning Laboratory](https://home.hiroshima-u.ac.jp/~mkt682/)
under Prof. Makoto Chikaraishi.

The subject is activity-based travel demand modelling with dynamic discrete choice
models — how a whole day of linked decisions about what to do, where, for how long
and by which mode can be modelled and estimated at the scale of a real city.
