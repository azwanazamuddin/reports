§0. Sample and likelihood

All figures below compare the 15-minute diaries of all 6,328 persons (2,626 workers, 3,702 non-workers) with 3 simulated days per person (18,984 simulated days). At θ̂ the simulation's graph scores the diary at LL = −39,090.68 on 6,299 persons and 366,459 steps; the estimator's canonical graph gives −39,095.94 on 366,470 steps (the difference is explained in the main report). LL(0) is still being computed; ρ² will be added when it finishes.
