The cost coefficient is now negative and clearly identified: −0.00222 per yen (SE 0.00012, t −19.2). On 10 November it was +0.00032 (t 1.9). Every travel time coefficient stays negative. The reading written before the run said: negative and more than 2 SE from zero means the commuting-allowance explanation holds in this model, as it did in the trip-level model (−0.00175, t −14). It does.

What changed from 10 November

Commute cost
A trip between home and work or school pays no money cost, for every mode: no fuel, fare or parking. Employers pay the commute (通勤手当, 91.7% of firms in 2014). Other trips pay as before.
Car passenger fuel
A car passenger pays no fuel; the driver does.
Commute as its own trip class
So that a commute can carry a different cost, a trip that leaves home for work (or work for home) is a separate class of travel state. The trip is still one choice of destination, mode and arrival activity; the model equals the 10 November one when commute cost is set back to paid, and this was checked on the full sample before the run (identical log-likelihood, gradient difference 8 × 10⁻¹⁶). The graph is larger: states +24%, edges +14%.
Trip scoring
A trip is scored as one decision (departure and arrival together), the only form that does not depend on how the trip is split into two edges. 183 fewer observed decisions than on 10 November (361,167 against 361,350), so the two log-likelihoods are not directly comparable.
Unchanged
Sample, activities, modes, the public transport timetable, the exact work block, opening hours, parking, the time-of-day grid and the reference (leisure at 14:00).

Convergence

  • Warm start from the 10 November estimate. The exact Hessian there (4.8 hours on 24 cores) has all 76 eigenvalues positive, smallest 13.8, and predicted a gain of 103 log-likelihood points; the run gained 175.
  • 11 evaluations, 8 iterations of a trust-region Newton method, about 7 to 8 minutes each. Evaluation 5 was a rejected trial step (−35,002, hollow point), after which the trust radius was cut from 2 to 0.5.
  • Stopped on the gradient test: projected gradient 1.3 × 10⁻⁶ ≤ 10⁻⁵. The last three evaluations agree to 2 × 10⁻⁵ log-likelihood points.
  • BHHH information at the estimate: all eigenvalues positive (smallest 12.9). Standard errors in this record are BHHH; an exact Hessian at the estimate was not computed.
  • A first attempt was stopped at 11:06 by the memory guard while another job ran beside it. The guard now stops competing jobs before the estimation.
