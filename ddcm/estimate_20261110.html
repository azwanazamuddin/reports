What this estimate specifies

Model
Dynamic discrete choice of the whole day in 15-minute steps (Västberg et al. 2020 form): stay or travel, where, by which mode, and what to do on arrival. Solved exactly by backward induction over a time-expanded graph; maximum likelihood on the diaries.
Sample
Weekday diaries, Higashihiroshima 2012/2014, persons aged 13 and over. People with unmodelled stops (hospital, pick-up and drop-off) or shuttle buses are left out.
Work and school
The work block is exact: a worker who arrives at work leaves after exactly the diary's duration (no overtime). The start time is the worker's own diary start; the workplace is the survey workplace. School is treated as work.
Activities
Home, work, shopping, leisure. Utility per minute varies by hour of day for home, shopping and leisure; shopping and leisure also by zone size. Leisure at 14:00 is the fixed reference (0). Shops close at their opening hours.
Modes
Car (driver), car passenger, train, bus, walk, bicycle. A time coefficient per mode group: car (driver and passenger share one), public transport, walk, bicycle. Constants per mode, plus train and bus constants for trips to or from outside the city.
Public transport
Times from the 2021–2025 timetable, door to door, by the hour of departure; a trip can only leave on a step with a departure. Walk to and from stations by distance per city.
Money cost
One cost coefficient for all modes. Car fuel 15 yen/km (driver and passenger); train and bus fares; parking for shopping and leisure by length of stay, none at work or home.
Within a tour
Walk ↔ car passenger switches allowed; public transport ↔ car passenger switches not allowed. A tour from home must reach an activity before returning home.

Open issue: the cost coefficient

Cost is +0.0003 per yen (t 1.9). It was negative only while car time was fixed at the household mode-choice value; with car time free, car time takes the distance effect (−0.080 per minute) and fuel, which is 15 yen per km, keeps little that is money.

The cause found since: commute trips are charged fuel, fares and parking that employers pay (通勤手当, 91.7% of firms in 2014). In the trip-level model, making commute trips free turns cost to −0.00175 (t −14). Car passengers should not pay fuel either. A model with both changes is being checked before it is estimated.
