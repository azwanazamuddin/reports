Part B. Observed vs simulated

Three sources for the same persons. The raw diary is the 15-minute diary. The diary as the model sees it is the same day after the encoder rebuilds it on the graph; this is what the likelihood scores. The simulation draws days from θ̂. Raw vs model-seen is what the model cannot represent; model-seen vs simulated is what it represents badly.

1. Which diary episodes never reach the likelihood?

A raw episode is kept when an encoded episode of the same activity for the same person overlaps it in time. The graph has no activity episode shorter than 30 minutes (the step that starts the activity, plus a minimum stay of one step), and the encoder merges a shorter stop into the next trip. This panel covers everyone; the group buttons do not apply. Work is a hard window (teleport), so its row shows what the minimum stay costs a fixed episode.
