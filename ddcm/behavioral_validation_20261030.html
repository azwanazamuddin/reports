§0 — Sample and likelihood

All figures below compare the 15-minute diaries of all 6,510 persons (2,684 workers, 3,826 non-workers) with 3 simulated days per person under the final θ̂ (19,530 simulated days). At θ̂ the simulation's graph scores the diary at LL = −42,459.14 on 6,487 persons and 376,761 steps; the estimator's canonical graph gives −42,462.12 on 376,773 steps (the 12-step difference is explained in the main report). LL(0) was not computed in this sharded run, so no ρ² is shown.
