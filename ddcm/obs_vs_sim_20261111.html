Three sources for the same persons. The raw diary is the 15-minute diary. The diary as the model sees it is the same day after the encoder rebuilds it on the graph; this is what the likelihood scores. The simulation draws days from θ̂. Raw vs model-seen is what the model cannot represent; model-seen vs simulated is what it represents badly.

1. Which diary episodes never reach the likelihood?

A raw episode is kept when an encoded episode of the same activity for the same person overlaps it in time. The graph's shortest episode is the step that starts the activity plus its minimum stay: 30 minutes by default, and 15 minutes for shopping and leisure when the run sets --min-stay to 0 for them (the 2026-10-28 re-spec does). The encoder merges a shorter stop into the next trip. This panel covers everyone; the group buttons do not apply. Work is a hard window (teleport), so its row shows what the minimum stay costs a fixed episode.

Car driver and car passenger apart

The mode panel above folds car passenger into car. Apart, per 1,000 person-days, raw diary, diary as the model sees it, simulation. The middle column is what the likelihood fits: a diary car-driver trip by someone whose household owns no car, or who holds no licence, is encoded as car passenger (estimation/observed_path.py; data_loader --driver-licence), which takes workers from 80 to 178 passenger trips per 1,000. Against that, the simulated 294 is 1.65 times, not 3.7: still the largest gap, but part of the distance to the raw diary is the encoding rule rather than the choice model.
