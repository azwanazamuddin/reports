5 Caveats and scope

  • Same-subsample discipline: observed stats are computed on the same worker subset used in estimation (persons filtered to those with WORK in their mandatory sequence, Motorcycle/Taxi excluded).
  • Null model: LL(0) sets estimated params (δ, α, β, β₁_shop, β₀_shop, β₁_leis, β₀_leis, θ_travel) to zero; fixed HH MNL constants (ASC, β_time, β_cost) are unchanged. This is the standard ρ² for this model family, not the uniform-path null.
  • 15-min step coarseness: simulated trip durations are multiples of 15 min. Observed trip durations from the CSV are exact (minute-level) — slight distributional mismatch at the lower end is expected.
  • Tour segmentation: observed tours are segmented from trip_data_higashihiroshima.csv using purpose='Home' for return trips. Simulated tours are segmented from TRAVEL/HOME alternation in simulated paths. Imperfect alignment in segment definition may inflate K-S.
  • Scope: This is in-sample validation — the same workers used for estimation. Out-of-sample validation requires a held-out subsample (future work).
